I presented a 90-day plan for modernizing retail stores with digital tools and AI.

- Replace mass marketing with personal, data-driven campaigns and real-time geofencing.
- Give in-store staff tablets with real-time customer data and recommendations.
- Use interactive WhatsApp receipts, gamified loyalty and AI copilots for customer service.

I closed by asking each retailer in the room to pick one of these ideas and prove its return on investment within 90 days.
