At the Retailers Association of India's Visakhapatnam Retail Summit, speaking for OptCulture, I gave store owners a 90-day plan for using digital tools and AI in three places: reaching customers, selling to them in the store, and bringing them back.

## Know and reach your customer

Mass marketing is expensive, dilutes the brand and does not build loyalty. The alternative starts with the sales data a retailer already has: AI can find customer groups hidden in it, and it can work as an early warning system for customers who are about to stop coming. Location helps too. With real-time geofencing, you can reach a customer when they are 500 meters from the store rather than five hours later.

## Engage and profit in the store

The store's staff can become trusted advisors if they have the right information in front of them. A tablet that shows a customer's purchase history and AI recommendations lets a salesperson say "I know you'll love this" and mean it. At billing, AI can suggest the best cross-sell to the cashier. And instead of flat discounts, offers can be personalized so they protect margins.

## Bring them back

The sale is the beginning of the next conversation. One WhatsApp message can carry the receipt, a short survey and a personalized catalog of recommendations. Loyalty can be more than points: personalized missions make it a game, and gamification gives customers a reason to choose you. And an AI customer service copilot, working inside WhatsApp, can resolve issues faster.

## The 90-day challenge

I closed by asking every retailer in the room to pick one of these ideas before the festive season and prove its return on investment on a single metric within 90 days.
