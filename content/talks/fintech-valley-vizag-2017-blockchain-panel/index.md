I was one of six panelists at the Fintech Valley Vizag Spring Conference on 9 March 2017, discussing how blockchain could transform the infrastructure that financial services run on.

Fintech Valley Vizag is the Andhra Pradesh government's initiative to build a fintech hub in Visakhapatnam, and the Spring Conference was one of its flagship events.
