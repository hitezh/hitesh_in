An alumni talk for IIBM Institute of Business Management, where I reflected on my Doctor of Business Administration (DBA) journey with IIBM and SIMA and presented a summary of my dissertation.

The research looks at how software companies price their products, and how they charge for AI features in particular. I studied 371 SaaS companies and compared four pricing approaches: subscription, usage-based, credit-based and hybrid.

Traditional subscription pricing is still the dominant model. 191 of the 371 companies use pure subscription pricing, and the next largest group, 67 companies, combines a subscription with usage charges. Only 11 are purely usage-based and one is purely credit-based. That pattern holds in every revenue band I looked at, from $1M to $100M in annual revenue, although subscription plus usage is a larger share among the bigger companies.

On packaging, tiered plans are the most common at 42% of companies, followed by hybrid packaging at 20% and custom or enterprise plans at 14%. Freemium accounts for 6%.
