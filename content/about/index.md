I am Dr. Hitesh Sarda. I advise leadership teams on AI: what to build, what to buy, what to skip, and how to prove a use case with a small pilot before anyone commits a budget. I run 256 Technologies, an applied AI lab in Hyderabad, and I wrote [Implementing AI Agents in Your Company](/book/), a free book for the managers who have to make this technology pay.

I came to that work the long way, through 25 years of building software that had to run.

## How I got here

It started with a computer my father could barely afford. He bought me an HCL Beanstalk PC during a difficult stretch for our family, and as I [wrote when I turned 42](/2020/42-do-i-have-all-the-answers/), I owe my career to him.

After an MBA I was placed from campus into the technology arm of a large global bank, and that job set the direction for everything after. It took me to Frankfurt for seven years, where I learnt how to build, run and support enterprise-scale systems, working up and down the stack long before "full stack" was a job title.

I have also done the opposite kind of work. I co-founded startups twice. At INSORCE, which sold technology to global financial institutions, I was the technical co-founder and CTO, which meant budgets, hiring, firing, architecture, product management and writing code in the same week. In 2014 I gave that job a name, the [Full Stack CTO](/2014/what-is-full-stack-cto/), and it is still my handle on X.

Since then I have worked across banking, payments, automation and retail, including production systems work at Citi, Virtusa, Yash and ValueLabs, and loyalty and marketing technology at OptCulture, where I am now an advisor.

## Early to the hard parts

I have a habit of getting interested in a technology before it is fashionable and writing down what I find.

- 2009: started this blog, and have kept it going since.
- 2011: finished Stanford's online machine learning class and [published my notes](/2011/machine-learning-my-notes./).
- 2017: was delivering robotic process automation to customers, and argued that [RPA is duct tape](/2017/rpa-the-golden-duct-tape/): useful, and no substitute for fixing the process.
- 2018 to 2020: wrote on GDPR, ISO 20022 and banking regulation, the unglamorous rules that decide what software in finance is allowed to do.
- 2026: [profiled in ET Focus, Hyderabad](#in-the-press), on India's AI opportunity, and published [the book](/book/) on putting AI agents to work in a company.

That pattern matters to the people I advise. I have watched several waves of technology arrive with the same promises, and I know what the gap between a demo and a production system costs, because I have worked on both sides of it.

## What I do now

Most of my work is helping a leadership team settle one AI decision at a time. It usually takes one of four forms:

- [AI advisory](/work-with-me/), starting with a single advisory hour.
- [Fractional Head of AI](/fractional-head-of-ai/), for companies that need senior AI judgment a few days a month.
- [AI training for leadership teams](/ai-training/), a six-week program built around the team's own work.
- [Speaking](/speaking/), at leadership offsites, industry events and engineering organizations.

My deepest experience is in banking, retail and loyalty. I am based in Hyderabad and work in person there, and remotely with teams across India and abroad. There is more on that on the [AI advisor in Hyderabad](/ai-advisor-hyderabad/) page.

## What I believe

A few ideas run through everything I write and every engagement:

- AI adoption is mostly an organizational problem, and the technology is the easier half.
- Most companies should buy before they build.
- An AI pilot exists to reduce uncertainty about the business, so design it to tell you the truth.
- The model is a commodity. Your data, your workflow and how fast your company learns are where the advantage is.
- Reward people for saying "I don't know". Confident wrong answers are what AI makes cheap.

The [guides](/guides/) collect the arguments behind these by theme: [what AI really costs](/guides/ai-cost/), [deploying AI agents safely](/guides/ai-agent-governance/), [leading teams that code with AI](/guides/ai-coding-agents/), [AI strategy for leadership teams](/guides/ai-strategy-for-leaders/) and [AI in retail and loyalty](/guides/ai-in-retail-and-loyalty/).
