I am Dr. Hitesh Sarda, an AI advisor based in Hyderabad, India, and the founder of 256 Technologies, an applied AI lab. I work at the intersection of AI research, practical engineering, and business outcomes, helping leadership teams navigate AI adoption with clarity and confidence.

My focus is on **Retail, Loyalty, and Industrial domains**, where I lead the advisory for AI implementation and consulting engagements. Each engagement ends with evidence-based recommendations your leadership team can actually act on.

I've been building and writing about software since 2009, from early experiments with .NET and Python to large-scale martech platforms, and now to the AI transformation reshaping every layer of the stack. That includes 25+ years of production systems work at Citi, Virtusa, Yash, and ValueLabs.

## What I do

I help organisations answer the hard questions around AI: what to build, what to buy, where the real risks are, and how to measure outcomes that matter. That means getting into the technical details when necessary and stepping back to the business case when that's what the room needs.

I work with companies in Hyderabad in person and with teams across India and abroad remotely, usually in one of three ways:

- **[AI advisory](/work-with-me/)**, often as a fractional Head of AI: strategy, feasibility, ROI, vendor assessment, and roadmaps.
- **[AI executive training](/ai-training/)**: a six-week program that makes a leadership team AI-native.
- **AI MVP development**: the smallest prototype that answers the key technical and business questions.

Current focus areas:

- AI strategy and implementation advisory for retail and loyalty businesses
- Agentic AI systems: design, evaluation, governance, and responsible deployment
- The real cost of AI: model choice, token spend, and when a smaller model is enough
- Translating AI research into practical engineering decisions
- Helping technical and non-technical leadership get on the same page

More detail on how I work with Hyderabad companies is on the [AI advisor in Hyderabad](/ai-advisor-hyderabad/) page.
