Most AI projects fail for boring reasons: the wrong use case, data that was not ready, or a vendor bet made before anyone validated it. An advisory hour is the cheapest way to avoid that.

Bring a problem or an idea. We will talk through the use case, your data readiness, and the real risks, and I will point you to the simplest pilot or MVP that can prove value, or tell you honestly if AI is not the answer yet.

I am based in Hyderabad and work with leadership teams in person across the city, and remotely with teams elsewhere in India and abroad. [More on working with me in Hyderabad](/ai-advisor-hyderabad/).

## What I can help with

- **AI advisory.** Strategy, feasibility, ROI modelling, vendor assessment and roadmaps grounded in real engineering constraints.
- **[Fractional Head of AI](/fractional-head-of-ai/).** Senior AI leadership for a few days a month, for companies not ready for a full-time hire.
- **[AI training for leadership teams](/ai-training/).** A six-week program with hands-on workflows, governance, and a 90-day adoption roadmap.
- **MVP development.** The smallest prototype that answers the key technical and business questions, across agents, computer vision and ML.
- **[Speaking](/speaking/).** Keynotes and workshops for leadership offsites and industry events.

## What happens after you write

1. I read every request myself and reply, usually within a couple of working days.
2. We fix a time for the advisory hour, in person in Hyderabad or on a call.
3. You leave the hour with the simplest next step. If that step does not need me, I will say so.
