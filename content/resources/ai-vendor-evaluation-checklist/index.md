A standard vendor questionnaire asks about certifications and uptime. Those matter, and they are not where AI purchases usually go wrong.

This checklist is written from the buyer's side, by someone with no product to sell. It covers the risks I write about: pricing that rests on subsidised compute, metering you cannot audit, a demo that measures the vendor's expert, a supplier who may ship your product, and what happens to your secrets when the vendor is breached.

## What is inside

- How to use it: send it before the demo, and what a refusal to answer tells you
- The questions, grouped by fit and proof, pricing, data and security, operations, agents, lock-in and the vendor's own business
- For each question, what a good answer sounds like
- A scoring sheet to compare up to three vendors
- Red flags
- What to put in the contract
