AI agents are the first kind of AI that acts instead of answering: they take a goal, plan the steps and work across your systems. That is where the value is, and also where a mistake stops being a wrong sentence and becomes a wrong transaction.

I help companies put agents to work without losing control of them. I wrote [Implementing AI Agents in Your Company](/book/), a free book for the managers who have to make this pay, and I write regularly about what happens when agents meet production: [how much autonomy to give them](/2026/the-autonomy-budget/), [how to limit what they can touch](/2026/coding-agents-blast-radius-job-title/), and [how to know afterwards what they did](/2026/what-your-agent-did/).

## What we work through

- **Where an agent belongs.** Which workflows have a business case and a result you can check, and which are better left to a person or a simpler tool.
- **Build, buy or wait.** Agent platforms, model choice and what the running cost looks like at production volume.
- **Permissions and blast radius.** What each agent may read, write and spend, and what enforces that limit.
- **A record of what happened.** Logs and reviews good enough to explain an agent's action to a customer, an auditor or a regulator.
- **A pilot that tells the truth.** One workflow, a baseline, a named owner, and an agreed test for going to production.
- **People.** Who supervises the agent, what they need to learn, and how the team's work changes.
