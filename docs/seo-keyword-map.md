look at who ranks. Autocomplete shows that people search a phrase; it does not
give monthly volumes. Replace this with Google Search Console data once the
site has a few weeks of impressions.

## Who ranks today (what we are up against)

- "AI consultant in Hyderabad": directories (Clutch, DesignRush, Twine), job
  listings and development agencies. No individual advisor ranks. A listing on
  those directories and a Google Business Profile matter as much as the page.
- "fractional Chief AI Officer India": almost nothing India-specific. The most
  winnable commercial phrase on this list.
- "AI speakers in India": speaker bureaus and event line-ups. Bureau listings
  and named past events are what move this.

## Gaps worth writing for (demand seen, no page yet)

- "AI consultant cost", "AI consultant hourly rate", "how much does an AI
  consultant charge": strong buyer intent. Needs Hitesh's real pricing model.
- "AI readiness assessment" (framework, template): a checklist or template
  would make a good download that collects leads.
- "chief AI officer salary in India": could be answered inside a post on when
  a fractional arrangement beats a full-time hire.
- "AI use cases for banking": fits his banking background; no guide yet.

## Deliberately not targeted

- "AI company / AI development company in Hyderabad": agency intent.
- Job and salary searches ("AI consultant jobs", "AI consultant salary").
- "AI SEO consultant": different service.
